# Sales Performance Dashboard — Excel

## Project Overview
This project analyzes sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify trends in revenue, profit, regional performance, and product categories. An interactive-style dashboard summarizes key performance indicators (KPIs) and business insights.

## Objectives
- Analyze monthly sales revenue trends.
- Compare revenue across regions.
- Identify the strongest product categories by revenue and profit.
- Calculate key performance indicators.
- Present findings through charts and a dashboard.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- Excel formulas
- Charts and data visualization
- Data quality checks

## Dataset
The practice dataset contains 500 sales records with fields including order date, region, city, sales representative, customer segment, product category, quantity, revenue, cost, and profit.

**Note:** This is a synthetic practice dataset created for portfolio and learning purposes. It does not represent actual company sales.

## Key Findings
- **Regional performance:** Central generated the highest revenue at ₹31,586.07, while North generated ₹25,165.46.
- **Category performance:** Furniture generated the highest revenue at ₹94,507.28 and the highest profit at ₹27,258.43.
- **Technology:** Generated ₹35,728.54 in revenue and ₹9,954.98 in profit.
- **Office Supplies:** Generated ₹6,924.24 in revenue and ₹1,936.60 in profit.
- **Monthly trends:** Monthly revenue varied during 2025, allowing further investigation of sales patterns.

## Business Recommendations
- Investigate the sales strategies and product mix contributing to Central's stronger revenue.
- Review product-level profit margins before prioritizing categories.
- Monitor monthly revenue trends to support sales planning.
- Track revenue and profit together when evaluating performance.
